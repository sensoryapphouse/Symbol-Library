BENZENE: Six medium carbon circles form a regular planar hexagonal ring, with six equivalent ring links and one outward small hydrogen circle per carbon. There are six carbons and six hydrogens, not twelve hydrogens. Each carbon has the introduced120degree trigonal-planar bond directions. It does not assert three permanently localised alternating long/short C–C bonds. This is one benzene representation, not two molecules for the source's label/rendition variants.

POLARITY COMPARISON: LEFT is bent water, one large oxygen and two small hydrogens, with an introduced104.5degree H–O–H angle. RIGHT is linear carbon dioxide, one medium carbon between two large oxygens, with double links. Two nearby water bond arrows point from hydrogen toward oxygen; the separate longer lower-left arrow shows their oxygen-side resultant direction. Equal opposed CO2 bond arrows also point from carbon toward oxygen and cancel; NO resultant arrow is drawn below CO2. Nonpolar CO2 still has polar C–O bonds. These are chemistry oxygen-directed polarity cues, not the SI electric dipole-moment vector convention, field lines, forces, motion, quantitative charges or electron trajectories. Arrow lengths are schematic. Triangles are arrowheads; no plus/minus or charge lettering is printed. The blank lower-right region is not missing atoms or an extra zero-shaped glyph.

HYDROGEN-BOND CLUSTER: Five water molecules: one central and four neighbours, with five large oxygen circles, ten small hydrogen circles, TEN solid covalent O–H links and FOUR segmented intermolecular hydrogen-bond tracks. Each water keeps exactly two covalent hydrogens. The central water donates two hydrogen bonds from its two hydrogens toward right-side neighbouring oxygens and accepts two from left-side neighbours' hydrogens toward its oxygen. A segmented track joins an oxygen and a hydrogen on ANOTHER molecule, never H–H or a third covalent hydrogen. In Index, separated short groups of carrier dots replace the visible short dash segments; the wider gaps carry the distinction from covalent carrier chains. This flat, legibility-spaced depiction introduces local tetrahedral coordination through two donor and two acceptor relationships; it is not a complete3D reconstruction or an exact instantaneous liquid-water network. Distances, orientations and apparent projected angles are schematic. It does not mean every water permanently has exactly four neighbours.
