Review of separate display adaptations

Symbol gallery · Collection report · Display adaptation review

42 meanings reviewed; no additional adaptation exports are claimed. 37 are unsuitable on both devices, and five only on Dot Pad. Their existing single-frame decisions remain unchanged. This is a per-item design proposal based on the retained fit assessments, not new tactile or hardware validation.

Recommendation: develop 34 multipart teaching candidates separately. For eight quantitative models, offer companion explanations while retaining the quantitative original; changing scale or encoding must be explicit. Originals and existing usable native versions remain available.

Every proposed frame needs its own pin-grid design and whole-frame inspection, preserved counts/connections, external accessible title/key/navigation, and eventual physical and learner testing. Any alternative must retain its parent meaning ID as a relationship and have a distinct adaptation/frame identity. A partial view must never be labelled the complete original diagram.

Alaska flag layout context

edu.alaska-flag-layout-context — monarch-display-v1, dotpad-display-v1

multipart teaching candidate

Existing limitation: The retained eight-star arrangement crowds on a 40-row grid. In the whole fit studies, enlarging the five-point outlines enough to preserve their form merges several neighbouring Big Dipper stars; shrinking to source-relative sizes loses the star forms. No layout preserving both the relative arrangement and distinct five-point symbols is supplied. Use the paper version. This concerns the assessed retained layout, not every possible separately taught redesign.

Proposed adaptation: Use a field/position overview and enlarged Big Dipper and Polaris details with matching orientation. A position-only overview must explicitly refer to the detailed star forms.

Acceptance requirement: Keep all eight star identities and their spatial relationships; explain any scale change rather than silently spreading stars.
