INDEX OUTPUT ROLES — 24 NOVEMBER 2026

These tags document authored geometry for future model-specific conversion.
They do not make an SVG a printer job or establish physical suitability.

data-output-role="graphic-dot": one intended individual dot at the circle
centre. The SVG radius is its drawn footprint, not a calibrated hardware size.
Retain its source-part IDs and any protected anchor information.

data-output-role="semantic-filled-region": the full painted region carries
meaning and must retain its extent. Existing "semantic-point-glyph" has the
same requirement; it is not a single physical punch. Examples include the
7.6 mm electron markers and solid-state regions. Printer conversion must
represent their area rather than collapsing them to their centre.

data-output-role="unraised-opening": preserve the opening and painter order.
Never fill this space merely because surrounding dots or areas are raised.

Preserve explicit millimetre width/height and viewBox offsets. Do not assume a
zero origin, change meaningful coordinates to fit a coarse grid, or shrink
documented oversized exceptions to paper automatically. Unknown geometry must
be reported, not silently omitted. Keep device-specific output separate from
the authored SVG and preview the actual output dots before physical testing.

The Utah revision consolidates 12 near-coincident pairs on the state boundary
and one overlapping pair at the inset connector/selection-box junction.
Surviving coordinates remain unchanged. data-merged-dot-ids records each
consolidated source ID; data-source-parts retains both parts at the junction.
Native shared vectors, city markers, dimensions, Swell and ViewPlus exports
are unchanged. This is an Index sampling repair, not a new geographical design.

Apply future edits to the Index layer of the editable master, regenerate the
export, update catalogue/checksums, and check other profiles for unchanged
output. Original-reference files in tactile/ remain preserved. Model, driver,
firmware, paper and physical/tactile checks are still required before declaring
an output suitable for a particular embosser.
