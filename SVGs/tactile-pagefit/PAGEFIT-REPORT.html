Limits and teaching context

This is an experimental educational collection. Source conventions, schematic counts, selected anatomy and geographical approximations are not universal facts or validated tactile teaching methods. Use an external accessible title, orientation, labels, explanations and teaching key. There is no visible text or Braille in the artwork. Page-fit teaching keys and the per-meaning catalogue explain revised designs; source limitations remain relevant except where an explicit new assessment supersedes an old dimension or drawing description.
