CYCLIC SUGAR MODELS — essential external teaching key

These are learned nontext Haworth-style node-and-bond diagrams. Keep the shown orientation. Atom circles are glyphs, not atomic radii or electron orbits. The main chemical cycle passes through five or six atom glyphs; the outline around each atom is not another chemical ring.
Carbon: an outline circle containing a central solid circular region. Oxygen: an empty outline circle. Each line between atom glyphs is one single bond. Do not count the fine Index carrier dots as atoms; each diagram has six carbon and six oxygen nodes.
Hydrogen is implicit: neutral carbon has four single-bond valences and neutral oxygen has two. Remaining valences carry H. The six carbon nodes carry seven H in total; the five terminal oxygen nodes carry one H each. Ring oxygen carries none. Each selected graph therefore represents C6H12O6, twelve heavy-atom bonds plus twelve implicit X–H bonds.
Haworth convention: the drawn ring is a schematic reference plane. A substituent bond towards page-top means above that plane; towards page-bottom means below it. This is a stereochemical convention, not a literal measurement of 3D angles. The C5-to-C6 hydroxymethyl group is above in all three D examples. The anomeric OH is on that same face: beta.
Fructose: beta-D-fructofuranose, PubChem439709. Ring order from its top oxygen clockwise: O–C2–C3–C4–C5–O. C2 OH above; C1 hydroxymethyl branch below C2; C3 OH above; C4 OH below; C6 hydroxymethyl branch above C5. The lateral OH on each terminal C1/C6 group does not introduce another stereocentre.
Galactose: beta-D-galactopyranose, PubChem439353. Ring order from upper-right O clockwise: O–C1–C2–C3–C4–C5–O. OH at C1 above, C2 below, C3 above, C4 above; C6 hydroxymethyl above C5.
Glucose: beta-D-glucopyranose, PubChem64689. Same ring numbering: OH at C1 above, C2 below, C3 above, C4 below; C6 hydroxymethyl above C5. Thus this pair differs at C4, not in formula or by a generic ring outline.
Circle-to-line contacts are intended atom/bond incidences. A carbon centre region and its surrounding contour jointly form ONE atom glyph. An oxygen node connected only to carbon is an OH node under the implicit-H rule. Numbering, source atom IDs and systematic R/S descriptors are provided below for the teacher; no letters or numbers are printed in the SVG artwork.
Swell: black raised-footprint intent on white. Index: explicitly dotted carriers, with filled carbon semantic regions kept distinct from fine line dots. ViewPlus: principal-line and point roles; actual device height codes and measured relief are not assigned. Shape relationships survive an all-black reading.
Nominal 640x640mm source guides are not page/device-fit or physical touch/recognition approval. Physical output, calibration, tactile legibility and learner recognition remain pending. These three selected cyclic/anomeric forms do not exhaust the forms sugars can take.
