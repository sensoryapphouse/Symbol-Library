These8visible residue glyphs across the comparison do not describe complete polymer sizes or universal branch spacing.
Each residue ring has five carbon corners and one small oxygen circle at its upper-right vertex. Starting just below that oxygen, the right-lower corner isC1, then clockwise lowerC2, lower-leftC3, upper-leftC4 and topC5. The stem aboveC5 leads to a small diamond assigned C6. The diamond is a positional marker, not another ring oxygen or an electron. Small circles inserted BETWEEN residues are glycosidic oxygens. Remaining hydroxyls/hydrogens and full stereochemical detail are omitted. This is a sparse Haworth-style linkage lesson, not a complete structural formula, measured conformation or molecular rendering.

The branch link leaves C1 of the right-hand upper branch residue downward through its bridge oxygen, then meets the C6 diamond of the central lower main residue. Its other main-chain linkage remains atC4; the branch is not an extra C1 donor on that main residue.
