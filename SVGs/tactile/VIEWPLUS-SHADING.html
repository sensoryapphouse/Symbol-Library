Why other diagrams remain unchanged

Examples include atomic shells, circuit conventions, empty vessels, routes, mathematical constructions and musical noteheads: filling a closed outline can change its meaning. Existing area cues are retained where already present. The detailed assessment records a reason for each unchanged meaning; these are design judgements, not claims of physical suitability.
