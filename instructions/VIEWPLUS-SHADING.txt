VIEWPLUS AREA SHADING — 6 NOVEMBER 2026

The 898-symbol tactile collection has been assessed for selective ViewPlus area
shading. 163 ViewPlus variants have new shading (including three initial animal
pilots); 735 retain their existing artwork. Some retained variants already have
grey area cues. An unfilled scientific, mathematical or structural diagram is
not automatically incomplete: its lines, spaces, points and selected regions
may already express the teaching target.

WHAT THE SHADING MEANS
Grey can distinguish an actual material/land area or a deliberately selected
region from its surroundings. Existing darker boundaries and important internal
features remain above it. Most additions use #808080 under black contours;
#bfbfbf is used beneath existing #555555 lines where appropriate. White is used
for background and deliberately unraised openings. No decorative shadows,
gradients, invented terrain levels or blanket colour-to-height mapping was added.
Existing contour coordinates, stroke widths and feature geometry were preserved.
New area geometry is editable and tagged inside the ViewPlus master layer.

The shape's actual fill and stroke shades are the printing input. Semantic
height-role attributes document intent; they do not themselves set printer
height. Darker graphics generally emboss higher with the ViewPlus grayscale
mapping, but these SVG shades are not calibrated heights in millimetres.
Do not apply outline-only conversion that removes the intended area fills.
The files deliberately do not imitate Index dot patterns. ViewPlus/Tiger's
output and emboss preview determine the dot layout for the selected device.

PRINT AND CHECK
Use the actual printer model, graphics mode, driver and paper settings. Preserve
grayscale through the print path and check Tiger's emboss preview. Print a small
representative selection before distributing the full set: area plus boundary,
a white hole in an area, close internal features and an unchanged line diagram.
Check height separation, readable boundaries, open spaces and paper condition
by touch with a qualified tactile reader/educator. Adjust for the learner and
teaching task. More raised levels are not necessarily more informative.

WHAT WAS VERIFIED
All 163 changed variants received individual whole-image screen inspection.
All 898 subjects received a catalogue/semantic/structure shading assessment;
this does not mean 898 new visual reviews. Individual reasons and exact current
export hashes are in VIEWPLUS-SHADING-ASSESSMENT.json. The retained decisions are
editorial judgements, not evidence that no alternative design could be better.
All 2694 tactile exports regenerated byte-identically from the current masters.
All 898 Index and 898 swell-paper exports remain byte-for-byte unchanged, as does
the complete visual collection. The shared masters changed only where needed
for the ViewPlus variant; use the current master for future regeneration.

Human review of the new shading, device calibration, actual tactile proofreading
and learner evaluation remain pending. Earlier acceptance of an outline version
does not constitute acceptance of its new shaded revision. This package is an
experimental testing resource, not certified or physically validated output.
